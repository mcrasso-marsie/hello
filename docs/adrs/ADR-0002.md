# ADR-0002: Pure word counter separated from command I/O

## Context

The architecture assigns the word rule to word-counter and all I/O to word-count-command; the rule must be testable in isolation.

## Decision

We will keep the word rule in a pure function hello.counter.count_words that performs no I/O, and keep all file, encoding, output and exit-status handling in hello.cli.

## Alternatives Considered

- Single module doing reading and counting together: rejected, couples the rule to the filesystem and makes it harder to test and reuse

## Consequences

### Positive

- The word rule is unit-testable without files
- Future utilities can reuse the counter

### Negative

- Two modules to maintain for a small feature

## Compliance and Verification

| Rule enforced | Type | Mechanism | Scope |
|---------------|------|-----------|-------|
| src/hello/counter.py imports none of os, sys, pathlib, io, builtins.open or subprocess | DETERMINISTIC | tests/arch/test_layering.py::test_word_counter_has_no_io_imports | word-counter |
